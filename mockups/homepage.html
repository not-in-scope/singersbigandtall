Our Story

A Family Business,
Over 50 Years Strong

In 1975, Nathan "Nate" Singer, his wife Fay, and their son Bruce opened the first Singer's Big & Tall in Oxnard — the beginning of what would grow into a small chain. Bruce has been part of it from day one, bringing over 50 years of experience to every customer he helps.

Today, Bruce and his wife Ellen run the San Luis Obispo store. It's a family shop where the person helping you knows the inventory, knows the brands, and genuinely cares how you look walking out the door. Basic alterations are included.
