Our Story

Realizing the need for a Big & Tall Men's clothing store in the Tri-Counties, my father, Nathan "Nate" Singer, along with my mom, Fay Singer, and myself, opened the first of what would become a small chain. It was 1975, the year I graduated from high school, that we opened the doors of Singer's Big & Tall in Oxnard. A rough start, my mom with a broken wrist and my dad recovering from eye surgery.

The store was well received in the community and the customers were grateful for the opportunity to "buy off the rack," clothing that fit.

Many years later, long after opening our seventh store, Nate passed away. Two years later, my mother passed away. Losing my parents was devastating to our family, business acquaintances, and the many customers they knew as friends.

I was faced with a huge decision. Do I carry on the family business that my father started so long ago? You bet! My wife Ellen joined me in the business and we are still a family business — the Mom & Pop of Big & Tall clothing stores — providing over-the-top customer service to all of my valued customers.

We hope you will come in and let us help you find what you're looking for! Thank you!

— Bruce Singer
