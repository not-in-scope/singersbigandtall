Our Story

SLO's Original
Big & Tall Store

Singer's was founded in 1975 by Nathan "Nate" Singer with one simple idea: every man deserves clothes that actually fit. Nearly 50 years later, his son Bruce and daughter-in-law Ellen carry on that tradition right here in San Luis Obispo.

We're a family shop where the person helping you knows the inventory and genuinely cares how you look walking out the door. We carry premium brands like Van Heusen, Perry Ellis, Cutter & Buck, Nike, and Carhartt in sizes that are nearly impossible to find at chain stores — and if something needs tailoring, basic alterations are included.
